About PyveJournal

PyveJournal is a love letter to the early-2000s blogging scene — LiveJournal, mixtape CDs and away messages — rebuilt with modern Python.

What you can do

  • Write entries with a little safe HTML (bold, links, lists, quotes…). Blank lines become paragraphs.
  • Set your current mood and music on every post, just like the old days.
  • Hide the long part of an entry with <lj-cut> — friends see a “Read more” link.
  • Choose who can read each entry: public, friends only, or private.
  • Add friends to build your friends page, and let them read your friends-only entries.
  • Talk in threaded comments, pick up to six userpics, and give your journal a style.

Under the hood

Flask 3 · SQLAlchemy 2 · Alembic migrations · Flask-Login · WTForms with CSRF protection · bcrypt password hashing · rate limiting · HTML sanitizing with nh3 · Pillow for userpics · PostgreSQL in production · pytest.

Built by Lucien Gaydos.
